Our Story

Cooked with fire,
served with pride.

AL-Faisal Fast Food Point started as a single grill in the heart of Old City, Bahawalpur — built on one promise: every plate leaves the kitchen exactly as it should, hot, honest and made from scratch. Today, that promise hasn't changed. Our chefs still hand-press every patty, stretch every dough, and marinate every skewer the same way we did on day one.
